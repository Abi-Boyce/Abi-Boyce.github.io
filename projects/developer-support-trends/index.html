Fourteen separate threads describe the same TestFlight failure, and each one is waiting on its own answer

I read the first two pages of the TestFlight tag and the first page of the App Store Connect tag on the Apple Developer Forums, the way I would read a support queue: tag each post, count, look for one cause behind many reports, and turn the biggest pattern into an engineering note, a help article and an automation.

Each post is tagged with one theme. The TestFlight cluster is highlighted because its symptoms match across accounts, which points to one cause rather than many user errors.

The pattern in the TestFlight cluster

Across the 14 posts, developers describe the same three symptoms, usually together, on every app in their account:

  1. Testers stay at "Invited" and never get the invitation email.
  2. Installs fail with "The requested app is not available or doesn't exist," even for the Account Holder.
  3. Submitting a build for external testing fails with HTTP 422 ENTITY_UNPROCESSABLE, sometimes BETA_CONTRACT_MISSING.

Five of the 14 posts bring up agreements on their own, and two tie the failure to accepting a new agreement in late October. Reported start dates run from October 20 to November 8. Production uploads keep working, so developers can ship to the App Store but can't beta test.

This is a pattern in public reports, not a confirmed cause. Only Apple's logs can confirm it.

What support ops could ship this week


1. One incident, not 14 cases


Group every case and thread with these symptoms under a single incident owner, link them, and reply to all of them at once when there is news. Developers in these posts say they waited days or weeks for a first reply.


2. A known-issue note


Known issue: TestFlight invitations and installs failing for some accounts
Some developers can't send TestFlight invitations, install builds or submit builds for external testing (HTTP 422). App Store uploads are not affected. We're investigating. You don't need to recreate groups, re-add testers or reinstall TestFlight. If you've opened a case, we've linked it to this issue and will update you here.


3. Better intake


Add a "TestFlight not working" path to the support form that asks for Team ID, the date the problem started, whether an agreement was accepted around then, and the request UUID from the error. Every post here had to volunteer this.
