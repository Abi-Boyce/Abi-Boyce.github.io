Fixes support can ship today


Macro: credits after a failed generation


Hi {first name}, thanks for flagging this, and sorry about the lost credits. A failed generation shouldn't use them, and I've returned {n} credits to your account. We've also seen this on other accounts since the latest update, and our team is fixing it. If it happens again, reply here with the time of the failed job and I'll sort it out right away.


Help doc draft: export stuck in Safari


Export stuck at 99% or download not starting?
1. Refresh the page; your finished video is saved in your assets.
2. Try the download in Chrome or Firefox.
3. Still stuck? Clear your browser cache, then try again.
4. Contact support with your browser version and the video's name.


The help doc lets the AI support layer resolve these tickets without a person.

Where the AI layer should hand off


  • Let the AI answer: how-to and plan questions that a help doc answers, like exports, plans and cancellations.
    • 

  • Send to a person: anything about money (refunds, charges), anything that repeats after a fix, and upset or confused users.
    • 

  • Send to Trust & Safety: content-filter disputes, since a wrong block and a right block look alike from the outside.
    • 

  • Send to Enterprise support: team and seat problems on business accounts.
