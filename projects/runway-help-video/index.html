A 40-second help video: "Did that failed generation cost you credits?"

One of the most common questions an AI video tool's support team gets is whether a failed generation used up credits. This video answers it in 42 seconds, so a creator gets the answer before opening a ticket, and an AI support assistant has something to link.

Sample, not an official Runway video. Facts checked against the Runway Help Center in November 2026.

The help article it sits in

Did a failed generation use my credits?

Short answer: if your generation ends in an error, your credits come back automatically within a few minutes. If it finished but isn't what you wanted, that isn't an error, so those credits stay spent.

How to check

  1. Open your credit usage history. Each charge and each return shows as its own line.
  2. If a generation errored and there's no return line after a few minutes, contact support.

Didn't like the result? Adjust your prompt or reference image and try again. Prompting tips are in the Help Center and the community Discord.

Contact support: profile icon, then Plans and Billing, then the help icon.

The reply macro

Hi {first name}, thanks for reaching out, and sorry that generation didn't work out. When a generation ends in an error, the credits come back to your account automatically within a few minutes, and you'll see the return in your credit usage history.

If the video finished but didn't come out the way you pictured, that isn't counted as an error, so those credits stay spent. This 40-second video walks through both cases: {link}.

If you had an error and the credits still haven't come back, reply here with the time of the generation and I'll look into it right away.

How I made it in under 2 hours

  1. Picked the question from Runway's Help Center articles on generation errors and credit refunds, the kind of ticket that repeats.
  2. Wrote a four-beat script: the worry, the error case, the "finished but not what I wanted" case, and how to check and get help.
  3. Built it with AI: I directed Claude to design the four illustrated scenes and animate them in code. I then reviewed every frame for overlapping text and readability on a phone.
  4. Checked every claim against the Help Center before publishing (sources below).
  5. Wrote the article and macro so the video works in a ticket reply and in self-service.

What I found along the way

I planned to animate the scenes in Runway. On a new free account, the header showed 500 credits, but every image and video model, including Gen-4 Turbo, asked me to upgrade. Runway's Help Center explains that a model showing an upgrade prompt isn't part of the free plan right now, while several outside pricing guides still describe free Gen-4 Turbo video.

That gap is a ticket waiting to happen: "I have credits, why can't I generate?" My next help article would answer it directly, and I'd flag the outdated third-party info to the content team.

What I'd measure
